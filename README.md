### About Me & Engineering Focus

My engineering foundation is built on C, Python, and physical hardware implementation. I have a strong background in Industrial Automation, Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC), and Control Systems. In my daily work, I specialize in agentic workflows. Instead of writing manual boilerplate code, I direct AI agents to rapidly architect, verify, and ship production ready web platforms and robotic control systems.

A primary application of this approach is my academic capstone project, ARES-01. It is an autonomous Mars rover engineered for remote inspection and physical manipulation. I built the system to unify onboard hardware control with browser based teleoperation, real time visual telemetry, multi axis robotic arm kinematics, and low latency Bangla and English voice commands. Alongside my robotics work, I also built Study Orbit. This is a full stack bilingual study platform for students featuring structured academic planning, focus timers, and AI assisted tutoring developed through agentic workflows.

I am currently in my 8th semester studying Electronics Technology at Barishal Polytechnic Institute. I am also preparing for the IELTS Academic exam (Target 8.5 to 9.0) to pursue international engineering research.
