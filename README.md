### About Me & Engineering Focus

My engineering foundation is in C, Python, and physical hardware implementation. I focus on connecting low-level embedded control with reliable software architecture. In my daily work, I specialize in agentic workflows—instead of writing manual boilerplate, I direct AI agents to rapidly architect, verify, and ship production-ready web platforms and robotic control systems.

A primary application of this approach is my academic capstone project, **[ARES-01](https://github.com/syedzayed-i2p/Ares_01)**, an autonomous Mars rover engineered for remote inspection and physical manipulation. I built the system to unify onboard hardware control with browser-based teleoperation, real-time visual telemetry, multi-axis robotic arm kinematics, and low-latency Bangla and English voice commands. Alongside my robotics work, I also built **[Study Orbit](https://github.com/syedzayed-i2p/Study_Orbit)**, a full-stack bilingual study platform for students featuring structured academic planning, focus timers, and AI-assisted tutoring developed through agentic workflows.

I am currently studying Electronics Technology at Barishal Polytechnic Institute and preparing for the IELTS Academic exam (Target: 8.5 - 9.0) to pursue international engineering research.
